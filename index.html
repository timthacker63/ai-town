AI Town

AI Town is a private, personal household assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service: it has a single user, its owner, and it doesn't accept sign-ups.

One of its helpers, "JP the postman", reads the owner's Gmail read-only, twice a day, and lists the messages that need attention: bills, appointments, deliveries, purchases, receipts and security alerts. It can't send, delete, label or change any email.

Privacy policy

AI Town uses Google sign-in only so the owner can grant read-only access to their own mailbox.
