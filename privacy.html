Privacy policy

AI Town · last updated November 11, 2026

AI Town is a private, personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, AI Town uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope gmail.readonly. It reads message headers (sender, subject, date) and message text from the owner's own mailbox. It never sends, deletes, labels, or changes email, and it doesn't access any other Google data.

How it uses the data

Twice a day, AI Town reads new messages and picks out the ones that need the owner's attention: things to do, bills, appointments, deliveries, purchases, receipts and security alerts. It shows a short list of these to the owner on their own computer. Receipts (merchant, date, amount, last four digits of a card) can be matched to the owner's own household budget.

To sort messages, AI Town sends the text of those messages to Anthropic's Claude AI service, under the owner's own Claude account. Before sending, it masks sign-in codes and long card or account numbers and shortens links. Claude is used only to sort and summarize the messages for the owner.

What it stores

Email text is deleted at the end of each run. AI Town keeps only short notes it writes about messages that need attention (for example "Electric bill due Friday, $120"), plus Gmail message IDs so the owner can open a message in Gmail. All of this is stored on the owner's computer. The Google sign-in token is stored on the owner's computer in a private, encrypted location.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The only outside service that sees message text is the AI sorting step described above, used solely on the owner's behalf.

AI Town's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove AI Town's access at any time in their Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services. Removing access stops all reading immediately; the owner can also delete AI Town's local notes from their own computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on AI Town's Google sign-in screen.
